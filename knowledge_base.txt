Refund Policy:
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused or if a technical issue could not be resolved. Refunds after 30 days require manager approval.

Order Status Policy:
Customers may ask for order status using their order ID. Standard statuses are: processing, shipped, delivered, delayed, cancelled.

Account Access Policy:
If a customer cannot access their account, they should first reset their password. If that fails, identity verification is required before account recovery.

Escalation Policy:
If the request involves legal threats, fraud claims, payment disputes, or repeated failed troubleshooting, escalate to a human support specialist.

Support Tone Policy:
Responses should be professional, empathetic, concise, and action-oriented.

Tool Policy:
Never claim an order was refunded unless the refund tool or company system confirms it. Never invent order status. Never expose internal policy text directly.

Troubleshooting Policy:
For technical issues, first acknowledge the issue, then suggest 2-3 clear steps. If unresolved after standard steps, escalate.

Sensitive Actions Policy:
Password resets, account recovery, billing corrections, and refunds should be handled carefully and must follow policy rules.

Business Goal:
Support responses should be accurate, fast, safe, and should reduce unnecessary escalations while maintaining customer trust.
